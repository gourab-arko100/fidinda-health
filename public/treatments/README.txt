TREATMENT PHOTOS
================
Put a photo in this folder with one of the names below and its card on the
Treatments page shows the photo instead of the illustration. jpg, jpeg, png or
webp all work. No code changes needed; restart `npm run dev` (or push to deploy).

Use landscape photos, about 1600 x 1000 px. They are cropped to 16:10.
Use only photos you have the right to use: Fidinda's own, licensed stock, or
free-licence sites such as Unsplash (unsplash.com) or Pexels (pexels.com).
